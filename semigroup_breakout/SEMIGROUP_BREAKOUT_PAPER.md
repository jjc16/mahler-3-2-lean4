## Abstract

When an AI-assisted formalization campaign attacks a long-standing open problem in number theory—such as Mahler's $3/2$ problem ($Z = \emptyset$)—does it merely interpolate inside the *convex hull* of known theorems, or does it explore the *semigroup* generated by known proof techniques? Based on a 226-file bottom-up Lean 4 campaign, we show that verifier-guided AI genuinely explores the **semigroup** generated by elementary algebraic, cocycle, and modular operations, discovering non-trivial results outside the published literature (such as the homogeneous centered-slack cocycle $\widehat{\Sigma}_N = \Sigma_N - 2^{T_N}$, the universal rung-shift identity $\widehat{\Sigma}_{N+1}(m+1) = 3\Sigma_{N+1}(m)$, and the Parry–Milnor–Thurston shift-domination law on the sub-ceiling corridor).

However, we prove a foundational meta-theorem explaining why that entire bottom-up semigroup hits a hard mathematical wall: **every generator in the elementary cocycle/congruence semigroup is invariant under replacing the natural numbers $\mathbb{N}$ by the 2-adic integers $\mathbb{Z}_2$**, where Mahler's conjecture is provably *false*. Worse yet, at any fixed finite horizon $K$, the integer state equations $2^{T_r} P_r = 3^{T_r} P_0 + A_{0,r}(s)$ and the real companion tail equations $2^{T_r} h_r = 3^{T_r} h_0 - A_{0,r}(s)$ **completely decouple** the integer seed $P_0 = 3 b_0 + 1$ from the real fractional offset $h_0$. Consequently, every Parry-admissible $K$-block word is realized by an actual positive integer $b_0 \in (0, 2^{T_K+1}) \subset \mathbb{N}$, which makes it mathematically impossible for any $K$-block auxiliary polynomial $F(P_0, \dots, P_K)$ to violate the archimedean/2-adic product formula $|Z|_\infty \cdot |Z|_2 \cdot |Z|_3 \ge 1$ using finite-horizon survival alone.

Armed with this **Post-Bit-Length Exhaustion Principle**—that any $\mathbb{Z}_2$-symmetry-breaking generator must explicitly use the positivity of the odd quotients $u_K \ge 1$ and the archimedean smallness of the seed $P_0 < 2^{T_K}$—we design and execute a cordoned-off 5-loop breakout campaign. We discover and formalize five new theorems in [`SemigroupBreakoutTheorems.lean`](./SemigroupBreakoutTheorems.lean) that are **false in $\mathbb{Z}_2$ and true in $\mathbb{N}$**:
1. **The Global Archimedean Rung Ceiling (`archimedean_rung_ceiling_and_stepSum_bound`):** For every positive seed $b_0 \in \mathbb{N}$ and every surviving block $K$, $2^{s_K} \le P_K$ and $2 \cdot 2^{T_{K+1}} < 3^{T_K}(2 P_0 + 1)$ (in particular, $2^{s_0} \le 3 b_0 + 1$).
2. **The Pure Power-of-3 ($u_K = 1$) Collapse & Mod-64 Trap (`unit_quotient_even_rung_instant_death_and_odd_rung_mod64_identity`):** Whenever $u_K = 1$ ($P_K = 2^{s_K}$, so $b_{K+1} = 3^{s_K - 1}$), every even rung $s_K = 2j$ dies on the very next step ($P_{K+1} \equiv 2 \pmod 8 \implies s_{K+1} = 1$), while every odd rung $s_K = 2j+1$ forces $s_{K+1} = 2$ and $4 P_{K+2} = 3^{2j+3} + 13 = 243(3^{2j-2} - 1) + 256$, trapping $s_{K+2} \ge 6$ into the congruence $256 \mid (3^{2j-2} - 1)$ ($s_K \equiv 3 \pmod{64}$), whose base case $s_K = 3$ ($b_{K+1} = 9 \to 21 \to 243$) dies in 3 blocks.
3. **Cross-Window Hankel Determinant Annihilation (`equal_step_window_hankel_determinant_identity_and_bound`):** Across any two windows $[r_1, r_2]$ and $[r_3, r_4]$ of equal step sum $d$, the $2 \times 2$ Hankel determinant $P_{r_1} P_{r_4} - P_{r_2} P_{r_3}$ annihilates the quadratic $\mathcal{H}_\infty^2$ term and satisfies $2^d(P_{r_1} P_{r_4} - P_{r_2} P_{r_3}) = P_{r_1} A_{r_3, r_4} - P_{r_3} A_{r_1, r_2}$ with strict archimedean sandwich $-3^d P_{r_3} < 2^{d+1}(P_{r_1} P_{r_4} - P_{r_2} P_{r_3}) < 3^d P_{r_1}$.
4. **Universal Two-Seed First-Divergence Valuation Law (`two_seed_first_divergence_valuation_and_bound`):** For *any* two seeds $b_1 < b_2 \in \mathbb{N}$ across arbitrary branches, their first binary parity divergence step $n_0$ equals the exact 2-adic valuation $v_2(b_2 - b_1)$ ($2^{n_0} \mid (b_2 - b_1)$ and $2^{n_0+1} \nmid (b_2 - b_1)$) with odd difference $g_{n_0}(b_2) - g_{n_0}(b_1) = 3^{n_0}((b_2 - b_1)/2^{n_0})$, forcing divergence before step $N$ whenever $b_2 - b_1 < 2^N$.
5. **Post-Bit-Length Unique Seed Pinning (`post_bitlength_unique_seed_in_dyadic_box`):** Once $T_N$ reaches the bit-length $W$ of $b_0 < 2^W$, $b_0$ is the unique integer in $[0, 2^{T_N+1})$ realizing the $N$-block prefix $s$.

## 1. Introduction: Convex Hull vs. Semigroup in AI Mathematics

A central question in modern mathematical AI is characterizing the reach of automated and semi-automated discovery:
- **The Convex-Hull Hypothesis:** AI models can only interpolate between existing proofs in the training literature.
- **The Semigroup Hypothesis:** When coupled with symbolic computation and a formal proof kernel (Lean 4), an AI can compose known proof generators $g_1, g_2, \dots, g_k$ into long chains $g_{i_1} \circ g_{i_2} \circ \dots \circ g_{i_m}$, reaching true, non-trivial theorems arbitrarily far outside the convex hull of human-written papers—**provided a path exists within the semigroup generated by $\{g_1, \dots, g_k\}$**.

Our 226-file bottom-up formalization of Mahler's $3/2$ problem demonstrated both the power and the limitation of the semigroup regime:
- **Outside the Convex Hull:** Chaining computational probing with Lean verification across 30 outer loops produced a complete, `sorry`-free theory of the sub-ceiling corridor ($0 < \Sigma_N < 2^{T_N}$), proving that it contains a unique, non-branching, strictly aperiodic infinite sequence $\kappa : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ governed by the rung-shift identity $\widehat{\Sigma}_{N+1}(m+1) = 3\Sigma_{N+1}(m)$ and the mixed shift-slack cocycle $\Sigma_{N+M}(s) = 3^d \widehat{\Sigma}_N(s) + 2^{T_N}\Sigma_M(\text{shift}_N s)$.
- **Inside a Closed Sub-Semigroup:** Despite composing generators 30 layers deep, every elementary generator used in those loops (ring identities, linear integer inequalities, cocycle composition, and mod-$2^k$/mod-$3^k$ congruences) holds not only in $\mathbb{N}$ but also in the **2-adic integers $\mathbb{Z}_2$** (and in non-standard models of Peano Arithmetic). Because the composition of two $\mathbb{Z}_2$-valid rules remains $\mathbb{Z}_2$-valid, the entire bottom-up semigroup is trapped inside the $\mathbb{Z}_2$-compatible sub-universe, where Mahler's conjecture is false by Hensel's lemma.

To break out of a closed sub-semigroup, one must enforce an explicit **symmetry-breaking filter**: every new generator must fail in $\mathbb{Z}_2$ and hold in $\mathbb{N}$ by exploiting **archimedean positivity and finiteness** ($1 \le u_K$ and $P_0 < 2^W$).

## 2. The Finite-Horizon Decoupling Meta-Theorem & Post-Bit-Length Free-Fall

### 2.1 Why No Local $K$-Block Auxiliary Polynomial Can Have Product-Formula Ratio $\rho(F) < 1$

In classical diophantine approximation (Thue, Siegel, Roth, Baker, Beukers), one constructs a non-zero integer $Z = F(P_0, \dots, P_K) \in \mathbb{Z} \setminus \{0\}$ that is divisible by a large power $2^a 3^b$ while bounded in real magnitude by $|Z|_\infty < 2^a 3^b$, violating the product formula $|Z|_\infty \cdot |Z|_2 \cdot |Z|_3 \ge 1$.

Why does every such local $K$-block auxiliary polynomial $F(P_0, \dots, P_K)$ fail to reach $\rho(F) = |Z|_\infty / (2^a 3^b) < 1$ when constrained only by $K$-block Mahler survival?

**Meta-Theorem 2.1 (Finite-Horizon Decoupling & Realizability Barrier).**  
*At any fixed finite horizon $K \in \mathbb{N}$ and for any block sequence $s = (s_0, \dots, s_{K-1})$ with $s_r \ge 1$:*
1. *Every integer boundary state $P_r = 3 b_r + 1$ ($1 \le r \le K$) is an affine-linear function of $P_0$ alone:*
   $$2^{T_r} P_r = 3^{T_r} P_0 + A_{0,r}(s).$$
2. *Every real companion tail $h_r = 3 \alpha (3/2)^{T_r} - P_r$ ($0 \le r \le K$) is an affine-linear function of $h_0$ alone:*
   $$2^{T_r} h_r = 3^{T_r} h_0 - A_{0,r}(s).$$
3. *The variables $P_0 \in \mathbb{N}$ and $h_0 \in \mathbb{R}$ are **completely decoupled** at horizon $K$: the archimedean survival conditions $0 < h_r < 1/2$ ($0 \le r \le K$) constrain only $h_0 \in \big(\frac{A_{0,K}(s)}{3^{T_K}}, \frac{A_{0,K}(s) + 2^{T_K-1}}{3^{T_K}}\big)$ (which is non-empty iff $\Sigma_r(s) > 0$ for all $1 \le r \le K$), while the integer block chain constrains only $P_0 \bmod 2^{T_K+1}$.*
4. *Consequently, for every Parry-admissible $K$-block word $s$, there exists a genuine positive odd integer $b_0 \in (0, 2^{T_K+1}) \subset \mathbb{N}$ and a genuine real number $\alpha = b_0 + \frac{1+h_0}{3} \in \mathbb{R}$ that survive all $K$ blocks. Because $(P_0, \dots, P_K) \in \mathbb{N}^{K+1}$ is realized by an actual tuple of positive integers, no integer polynomial $Z = F(P_0, \dots, P_K)$ can violate $|Z|_\infty \ge 2^a 3^b$ at horizon $K$ without using an additional hypothesis that excludes $b_0 \in (2^W, 2^{T_K+1})$—namely, the **post-bit-length hypothesis $P_0 < 2^{T_K}$**.*

## 3. Five Formalized $\mathbb{Z}_2$-Symmetry-Breaking & Breakout Theorems

In [`SemigroupBreakoutTheorems.lean`](./SemigroupBreakoutTheorems.lean), we formalized five theorems that explicitly break the $\mathbb{Z}_2$ / single-branch symmetry of the bottom-up campaign:

### 3.1 Theorem SB.1: The Global Archimedean Rung Ceiling (`archimedean_rung_ceiling_and_stepSum_bound`)

In $\mathbb{Z}_2$, a 2-adic integer $b_0 \in \mathbb{Z}_2$ can have arbitrarily large 2-adic valuation $s_K = v_2(3 b_K + 1)$ at any step $K$ (for example, $b_0 = (2^{1000} - 1)/3 \in \mathbb{Z}_2$ has $s_0 = 1000$). In $\mathbb{N}$, however, the odd quotient $u_K = P_K / 2^{s_K}$ is a **positive** odd integer ($u_K \ge 1$), which couples with the renewal master equation $2^{T_K} P_K = 3^{T_K} P_0 + A_{0,K}(s)$ and the survival condition $2 A_{0,K}(s) < 3^{T_K}$ to yield:
$$2 \cdot 2^{T_{K+1}} \le 2 \cdot 2^{T_{K+1}} u_K = 2 \cdot 3^{T_K} P_0 + 2 A_{0,K}(s) < 3^{T_K}\big(2 P_0 + 1\big).$$
Unlike the sub-ceiling bound $s_K \le 2 T_K + 2$ (which applies only while $\widehat{\Sigma}_{K+1} < 0$), Theorem SB.1 bounds $s_K$ and $T_{K+1} = T_K + s_K$ across **all** surviving blocks—even those that restore the full ceiling—and at $K = 0$ gives $2^{s_0} \le 3 b_0 + 1$.

### 3.2 Theorem SB.2: The Pure Power-of-3 ($u_K = 1$) Collapse & Mod-64 Trap (`unit_quotient_even_rung_instant_death_and_odd_rung_mod64_identity`)

The archimedean ceiling $2^{s_K} \le P_K$ is sharpest when $u_K = 1$ ($P_K = 2^{s_K}$), which forces the next odd state to be a pure power of $3$: $b_{K+1} = 3^{s_K - 1}$. We proved:
1. **Even Rung ($s_K = 2j, j \ge 1$):** $b_{K+1} = 3^{2j-1}$ has $P_{K+1} = 3^{2j} + 1 = 9^j + 1 \equiv 2 \pmod 8$, so $v_2(P_{K+1}) = 1$ (**instant odd-to-odd death on the very next block**).
2. **Odd Rung ($s_K = 2j+1, j \ge 1$):** $b_{K+1} = 3^{2j}$ has $P_{K+1} = 3^{2j+1} + 1 = 3 \cdot 9^j + 1 \equiv 4 \pmod 8$, forcing a short block $s_{K+1} = 2$ with $u_{K+1} = (3^{2j+1} + 1)/4$ and $b_{K+2} = 3 u_{K+1}$. Remarkably, $P_{K+2} = 3 b_{K+2} + 1$ obeys the exact algebraic identity:
   $$4 P_{K+2} = 3^{2j+3} + 13 = 243\big(3^{2j-2} - 1\big) + 256.$$
   Because $\gcd(243, 256) = 1$, the post-`2` sub-ceiling recovery condition $s_{K+2} \ge 6$ ($64 \mid P_{K+2} \iff 256 \mid 4 P_{K+2}$) holds **if and only if**
   $$256 \;\Big|\; \big(3^{2j-2} - 1\big) \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad 64 \;\Big|\; (2j - 2) \quad\big(s_K \equiv 3 \pmod{64}\big).$$
3. **Collapse of the Base Case $j = 1$ ($s_K = 3, b_{K+1} = 9$):** At $j = 1$, $3^{2j-2} - 1 = 0$, so $4 P_{K+2} = 256 \implies P_{K+2} = 64 = 2^6 \cdot 1$, which has $s_{K+2} = 6$ (even) and $u_{K+2} = 1$—immediately triggering the Even-Rung Collapse at $b_{K+3} = 3^5 = 243$ ($P_{K+3} = 730 \equiv 2 \pmod 8 \implies s_{K+3} = 1$)!

### 3.3 Theorem SB.3: Cross-Window Hankel Determinant Annihilation (`equal_step_window_hankel_determinant_identity_and_bound`)

Across any two block windows $[r_1, r_2]$ and $[r_3, r_4]$ of equal step sum $d = T_{r_2} - T_{r_1} = T_{r_4} - T_{r_3}$, the $2 \times 2$ Hankel determinant $\det \begin{pmatrix} P_{r_1} & P_{r_2} \\ P_{r_3} & P_{r_4} \end{pmatrix} = P_{r_1} P_{r_4} - P_{r_2} P_{r_3}$ annihilates the leading $\mathcal{H}_\infty^2 (3/2)^{T_{r_1} + T_{r_4}}$ growth term and satisfies the exact identity
$$2^d \big(P_{r_1} P_{r_4} - P_{r_2} P_{r_3}\big) = P_{r_1} A_{r_3, r_4} - P_{r_3} A_{r_1, r_2},$$
forcing $2^d \mid (P_{r_1} A_{r_3, r_4} - P_{r_3} A_{r_1, r_2})$ and, whenever both windows survive ($2 A_{r_1, r_2} < 3^d$ and $2 A_{r_3, r_4} < 3^d$), trapping the determinant in the open archimedean interval:
$$-3^d P_{r_3} < 2^{d+1}\big(P_{r_1} P_{r_4} - P_{r_2} P_{r_3}\big) < 3^d P_{r_1}.$$

### 3.4 Theorem SB.4: Universal Two-Seed First-Divergence Valuation Law (`two_seed_first_divergence_valuation_and_bound`)

Whereas Campaign 2 compared two seeds $b_1 < b_2$ only when they shared the *same* block sequence $s$, Theorem SB.4 analyzes *arbitrary* seed pairs $b_1 < b_2 \in \mathbb{N}$ at their **first binary parity divergence step $n_0$** ($\forall k < n_0,\, \operatorname{orbit}(b_1, k) \bmod 2 = \operatorname{orbit}(b_2, k) \bmod 2$ and $\operatorname{orbit}(b_1, n_0) \bmod 2 \ne \operatorname{orbit}(b_2, n_0) \bmod 2$):
- The orbit difference $D_{n_0} = \operatorname{orbit}(b_2, n_0) - \operatorname{orbit}(b_1, n_0)$ is an **odd** positive integer (`D % 2 = 1`),
- $2^{n_0} D_{n_0} = 3^{n_0}(b_2 - b_1)$, so **$2^{n_0} \mid (b_2 - b_1)$ and $2^{n_0 + 1} \nmid (b_2 - b_1)$**: the first divergence time $n_0$ is **literally the 2-adic valuation $v_2(b_2 - b_1)$**,
- Consequently, any two seeds $b_1 < b_2$ with $b_2 - b_1 < 2^N$ **must diverge at some step $n_0 < N$**, and at step $n_0$ their companion fractional parts split into the disjoint intervals $[0, 1/3)$ (even branch) and $[1/3, 1/2)$ (odd branch).

### 3.5 Theorem SB.5: Post-Bit-Length Unique Seed Pinning (`post_bitlength_unique_seed_in_dyadic_box`)

Whenever $b_1, b_2 < 2^{T_N + 1}$ both realize an $N$-block prefix $s$ ($N \ge 1$), $b_1 = b_2$. Thus, once the elapsed binary steps $T_N$ exceed the bit-length $W$ of a fixed seed $b_0 < 2^W$, $b_0$ is the unique integer in $[0, 2^{T_N+1})$ compatible with $s$, forcing all top $(T_N + 1 - W)$ binary bits of the 2-adic Hensel lift $(-A_{0,N}(s) \cdot 3^{-T_N}) \bmod 2^{T_N+1}$ to vanish simultaneously.
