\begin{abstract}
Mahler's $3/2$ problem (1968) asks whether there exists any real ``$Z$-number'' $\alpha > 0$ such that the fractional part $\{(3/2)^n \alpha\}$ lies in $[0, 1/2)$ for every integer $n \ge 0$. Writing $\alpha = a_0 + \xi$ with $a_0 = \lfloor \alpha \rfloor \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\xi \in [0, 1)$, survival up to step $N$ is equivalent to the discrete ceiling recurrence $g_{n+1} = \lceil 3 g_n / 2 \rceil = \lfloor (3 g_n + 1)/2 \rfloor$ ($g_0 = a_0$) admitting a non-empty intersection of $N$ nested archimedean companion intervals $[\mathrm{lowerBound}(a_0, n),\, \mathrm{upperBound}(a_0, n))$ (\texttt{InfiniteZNumberBridgeAndRationalBase.lean}).

In this work, we present a 142-file, \texttt{sorry}-free formalization in Lean~4 that investigates both \textbf{constructive survival} and \textbf{obstructions to infinite orbits}:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Exact 1D Slack Reduction \& Infinite-Horizon $Z$-Number Bridge:} By grouping each odd step $g \mapsto (3g+1)/2$ with its $s(k)-1$ subsequent even steps $g \mapsto 3g/2$ via $3 b_k + 1 = 2^{s(k)} u(k)$ ($\gcd(u(k), 6) = 1$), we collapse all $\binom{K}{2}$ pairwise companion-interval inequalities along a finite block chain into a \textbf{single 1D recurrence} (\texttt{minSlack}), and prove its exact two-way equivalence with the existence of a real companion (both in interval form and direct \texttt{Int.fract} form). Normalizing by $2^{T(m)}$ yields the time-homogeneous 1D slack map $\mu_2(s, m+1) = \min\big(1,\, (3/2)^{s(m)} \mu_2(s, m) - 2\big)$ driven by the arithmetic-controlled block length $s(m) = v_2(3 b_m + 1)$ (\texttt{CoupledFractionalPartDynamics.lean}, \texttt{FiniteSeedCarryAndBackwardDescent.lean}).
  \item \textbf{Constructive Survivors \& 113-Step Record-Holder:} We prove that any block sequence with $s(k) \ge 3$ saturates the ceiling $\mathrm{minSlack}\, s\, m = 2^{T(m)}$ and automatically admits a valid real companion at every finite horizon. Using this sufficiency theorem, we construct closed-form seed families surviving arbitrarily many blocks prior to ceiling exit, and machine-certify a 55-bit integer ($\mathrm{recordHolderA0} = 29{,}871{,}555{,}608{,}238{,}677 < 2^{55}$) that survives \textbf{113 steps} (58 steps past its 55-bit binary length) before dying at step 114 (\texttt{MahlerGrandSynthesis.lean}, \texttt{InfiniteZNumberBridgeAndRationalBase.lean}).
  \item \textbf{Unconditional Exclusion of Restricted \& Aperiodic Classes:} We prove explicit, logarithm-free step cutoffs in Lean~4 ruling out: (i) sub-ceiling bounded-block runs ($s(k) \le c$), (ii) periodic and uniform-macro-block sequences ($2^{D m} \mid ((3^D - 2^D)(3a_0+1) + 2^{s(0)} R_0)$), (iii) \textbf{aperiodic ceiling-saturated Sturmian and $(L+C_0)$-complexity sequences} in $\{3, 4\}^{\mathbb{N}}$ ($3 L \le 12 C_0 + 5 W + 2$) and $[3, 5]^{\mathbb{N}}$ ($L \le 50 C_0 + 17 W + 9$), and (iv) \textbf{general sub-critical balanced complexity sequences} ($\kappa \le (17-\delta)/10 < 1.7$, $3 \delta L \le D_0 + 17 W + 9 + \delta$, via our $17:10$ weighted repetition cutoff and the Finite Pigeonhole Principle in \texttt{SturmianAndBalancedComplexityExclusion.lean}). We also formalize the exact arithmetic setup for Baker--Matveev, Ridout, and Schlickewei Subspace analyses, separating the machine-checked algebraic/analytic bounds from external transcendence theorems not yet in Mathlib.
  \item \textbf{Finite Positive Seeds vs.\ 2-Adic Paths \& Certified Structural Barriers:} We prove \emph{why} symbolic/2-adic survivors exist in $\mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{R}$ while failing for positive integers $a_0 \in \mathbb{N}$: the partial sums $\Sigma_m(s) = \sum_{k=1}^m (2/3)^{T(k)+1}$ converge in $\mathbb{R}$ to $(0, 1/3]$ while converging in $\mathbb{Q}_2$ to the negative rational $-\frac{6a_0+2}{3} \le -2/3 < 0$ (\texttt{SubstitutionMahlerMethodAndGrandSynthesis.lean}). In particular, we resolve the $G_{3,6}$ product-graph false-positive cycle $(5,5) \to (4,2) \to (6,3) \to (5,5)$ ($a_0 = -9/19 \in \mathbb{Z}_2$ vs.\ $8^m \mid (19 a_0 + 9)$ and $3m \le W + 4$ for $a_0 < 2^W$) and establish the backward-lifting descent dichotomy (\texttt{FiniteSeedCarryAndBackwardDescent.lean}).
\end{enumerate}
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and Literature Attribution}

When attempting to prove Mahler's $3/2$ conjecture or related Collatz-type problems, informal proof sketches typically fall into two traps: (1) treating 2-adic parity constraints and archimedean interval constraints as statistically independent, or (2) constructing auxiliary linear combinations, Wronskians, or multi-origin determinants without noticing that the underlying 1D affine recurrence forces those combinations to be algebraically dependent. By formalizing every proposed attack vector in Lean~4, we separated genuine number-theoretic structure from mirages.

\section{The Odd-Block Reduction \& Dual 2-Adic / 3-Adic Recurrence}

A sequence of block lengths $s : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ and odd quotients $u : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N}$ forms a \texttt{ValidBlockChain} $a_0\, s\, u\, K$ if for every $k < K$:
\[
  1 \le s(k), \qquad u(k) \bmod 2 = 1, \qquad 3 \cdot b_k + 1 = 2^{s(k)} u(k),
\]
where $b_k := \mathrm{orbit}\, a_0\, (T(k))$ and $T(k) := \mathrm{blockSteps}\, s\, k = \sum_{i=0}^{k-1} s(i)$. Across consecutive blocks ($k + 1 < K$), the quotients and orbit boundaries satisfy:
\[
  2^{s(k+1)} u(k+1) = 3^{s(k)} u(k) + 1, \qquad b_{k+1} = 3^{s(k)-1} u(k), \qquad \gcd(u(k), 6) = 1.
\]
Defining $R(s, 0) = 0$ and $R(s, m+1) = 3^{s(m)} R(s, m) + 2^{\mathrm{tailSteps}\, s\, m}$, the intrinsic integer slack satisfies:
\[
  \Delta_m := \mathrm{integerSlack}\, s\, m := 3^{T(m)} - 2^{s(0)+1} R(s, m) = 3^{T(m)+1}(2a_0 + 1) - 2^{T(m+1)+1} u(m).
\]
